Agent Keyboard

Your own Agent Keyboard, running on a Fly.io Sprite.

Four steps, no terminal. Everything runs in your Fly account with your Claude subscription. We only set it up.

What we keep: your GitHub email and login, the repo you pick, and an encrypted Fly credential that lets the dashboard restart, update or delete your Sprite. You can revoke it any time in your Fly dashboard under Tokens ("Agent (auth.md)"). Your Claude token goes into a connector in your Fly account and never reaches us. A light instance costs about $1/month on Fly. New Fly accounts get $1 of credit for 7 days, then need a card.
